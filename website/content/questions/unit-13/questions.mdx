**Select Questions to Download.** Selected questions will be combined into a LaTeX (Beamer) or
Typst file that you may compile on your local machine; choose the language beside the **Download**
button, or with the switch at the foot of the sidebar. A Typst file includes its images, so it is a
single file, and each question's Typst source can be edited in the browser with a live preview.
